# Terms of Use

**for the use of the Consenter Manager and the Consenter Cookie Banner (for communication with the Consenter Consent Agent)**

**June 2026**

Law & Innovation Technology GmbH operates a trust service through which data protection-related decisions can be requested or made, and in particular where informed consent can be obtained or given (hereinafter referred to as 'Consenter').

Consenter was developed on the basis of an interdisciplinary research and development process, the aim of which is to incorporate and further develop state-of-the-art technology for the effective implementation of informed consent in accordance with Articles 25 and 32 of the GDPR. Insofar as technically possible and economically reasonable, the design of Consenter is based on empirically proven methods for increasing the effectiveness of transparency, consent and control mechanisms.

Consenter comprises the Consenter Cookie Banner, which customers can configure via the Consenter Manager and which can communicate with the Consenter Consent Agent via a technical API and the so-called transfer notification, and the Risk Assessment Centre (see the individual components of the service on the website [www.consenter.eu/de](http://www.consenter.eu/de)). To this end, Consenter processes the personal data of the customer's end users on behalf of the customer (hereinafter the 'Data Controller'). This agreement governs the cooperation between Consenter and the customer (hereinafter also referred to as the 'Agreement').
